FluteEcho is a microphone passthrough app with echo/delay and automatic feedback suppression, designed for transverse flute practice.

Connect your phone to a Bluetooth speaker, open the app, and play — the app monitors the audio in real time, detects feedback frequencies and applies notch filters automatically.

Features:
• Mic passthrough with adjustable echo and delay
• Room and Cathedral presets
• Real-time spectrum analyzer (flute range highlighted)
• Auto feedback suppression (up to 18 kHz)
• Frequency log with feedback markers
• No internet required, no tracking, no ads
